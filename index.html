HOW TO PLAY

A D or ← →

Steer & Carve

Carve left and right down the slope. Swerve around pine trees and jagged boulders.

W or ↑ or Shift

Aerodynamic Tuck

Crouch low to minimize drag, reach blazing speeds, and earn speed multiplier points.

SPACE

Ollie / Jump

Launch off snow or kicker ramps. Land cleanly to score massive air-time bonus points!

Q E

Air Spins

Hold Q or E in the air to spin 180°–720°. Land facing forward or switch for points!

S

Board Grabs

Hold S mid-air for Method and Indy grabs. Release before landing to avoid a wipeout!

R   P / Esc

Restart & Pause

R restarts or respawns at your last checkpoint. P or Esc pauses the game at any time.

PRO TIPS

  • ✨ Trick Combos: Combine a 360° spin with an S grab for a 1.5× mega trick multiplier!
  • ⚠️ Landing Alignment: Land facing straight or switch. Sideways landings cause edge-catch wipeouts!
  • 🏁 Checkpoints: Pass glowing blue gates for +250 pts and a safe respawn anchor.
  • ⚡ Nitro Boost: Grab glowing cyan canisters for a 4-second supersonic speed rush!
  • 🪙 Gold Coins: Gather glowing coin lines & arcs for +10 coins and +50 points each.
  • 🎯 Slalom Gates: Pass between red and blue flags for +500 bonus points each.
  • 📱 Mobile: Use the on-screen touch buttons at the bottom to steer, jump, grab & spin!
