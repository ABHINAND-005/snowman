HOW TO PLAY

A D or ← →

Steer & Carve

Carve left and right down the slope. Keep your balance and swerve around pine trees and jagged boulders.

W or ↑

Aerodynamic Tuck

Crouch down low into a speed tuck to minimize drag, reach blazing speeds, and earn speed multiplier points.

SPACE

Ollie / Jump

Launch off the snow or kicker ramps into the air. Land cleanly to score massive air-time bonus points!

Q E

Air Spins

Hold Q or E in the air to spin 180°, 360°, 540°, or 720°. Time your rotation to land facing forward or switch!

S

Board Grabs

Hold S in mid-air for stylish Method and Indy grabs. Hold longer for bigger points, but release before touching down!

PRO TIPS

  • ✨ Trick Combos: Combine a 360° spin with an S grab for a 1.5x mega trick multiplier!
  • ⚠️ Landing Alignment: Land facing straight or switch. Landing sideways or holding a grab causes an edge-catch wipeout!
  • 🏁 Checkpoints: Pass glowing blue gates for +250 pts and a safe respawn anchor.
  • ⚡ Nitro Boost: Grab glowing cyan canisters for a 4-second supersonic speed rush!
  • 🪙 Gold Coins: Gather glowing coin lines & arcs for +10 coins and +50 points each.
  • 🎯 Slalom Gates: Pass between red and blue flags for +500 bonus points each.
  • 🚀 Snow Kickers: Hit natural snow ramps at high speed for enormous jump air-time.
  • 🌲 Danger Ahead: Watch out for pine trees, boulders, fallen logs, ice blocks, and snowmen!
